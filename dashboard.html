How NekoNotes works

Three small habits, one growing sticker book.

1

Add your tasks

Jot down what you need to do and pick how big it is. Bigger tasks pay out more paws.

2

Keep your diary

Write about your day, choose a mood, and earn bonus paws for your first entry each day.

3

Unlock cats

Spend your paws on a mystery sticker. Ten collectible cats are waiting, from sweet to silly.
